Happening around this time

    Why ice ages happen

    Earth cools when less heat is trapped or more sunlight is reflected. Earth has frozen at least eight times, and each glaciation had its own mix of causes. Pick a cause or an ice age to see how they connect.

    The albedo feedback loop

    Once ice starts to build, it speeds up its own growth. That is how the Cryogenian ice reached the equator.

    Causes

      The supercontinent cycle

      Continents gather, insulate the mantle beneath them, then rift apart above superplumes. One full Wilson cycle takes a few hundred million years.

      1. SuperplumeHeat builds at the core–mantle boundary under the insulating supercontinent.
      2. RiftingRift valleys form along triple junctions.
      3. New oceanSea-floor spreading opens an ocean with passive margins.
      4. SubductionOld ocean crust breaks off and sinks; the ocean starts to close.
      5. CollisionArcs, terranes and continents collide in orogenies.
      6. New supercontinentThe pieces reassemble, and the cycle repeats.

      How rocks are dated

      Relative dating puts layers in order. Absolute dating gives a number: tree rings, paleomagnetism, index fossils and, for old rocks, radioactive decay inside minerals such as zircon.
